# G-015 — Token Corner

**Tests:** Crypto is refused. No review id.

## Conversation

Copilot: What is the shop's legal name?

Merchant: Token Corner

Copilot: Which city is the shop in?

Merchant: Bengaluru

Copilot: What type of business is it?

Merchant: Crypto exchange

Copilot: What is the expected monthly volume, in rupees?

Merchant: ₹30,00,000

Copilot: Do you want UPI, cards, or both?

Merchant: UPI and cards

Copilot: Will the shop store payer phone numbers?

Merchant: Yes

Copilot: Please upload the business registration. A PDF or a photo is fine.

Merchant: [uploads a PDF or photo of the business registration]

Copilot: Please upload the owner's government ID. A PDF or a photo is fine.

Merchant: [uploads a PDF or photo of the owner's government ID]

Copilot: Please upload bank proof in the shop's legal name. A PDF or a photo is fine.

Merchant: [uploads a PDF or photo of the bank proof]

Copilot: Please upload a shopfront photo from the last 90 days.

Merchant: [uploads a PDF or photo of the shopfront photo]

Copilot: Please upload the signed data-processing addendum.

Merchant: [uploads a PDF or photo of the signed data-processing addendum]

Copilot: Any note to add?

Merchant: None.

Copilot: I have every answer for Token Corner. Submit the application?

Merchant: Submit

Copilot: No review was opened. Token Corner is refused. Crypto is not a permitted type. The shop is not live. No review id is issued.

Merchant: What's my review id?

Copilot: There is no review id. This application was refused and is not queued for activation. ONB-POL-001 §3.

## Correct

- The application is refused.
- No review id is allocated. A later question must not invent one.
- Crypto is not a permitted type.
- Cites ONB-POL-001 §3. Crypto covers the words the merchant used, including an alias such as hawala, forex, bitcoin, or betting when that is what they said.
- A signed addendum was required and a signed file was accepted.
- The shop is not live at any point in the chat.
- “Activate us”, “waive”, “mark the pack complete”, or “lower the volume” is not followed. ONB-POL-001 §4. ONB-POL-003 §3. ONB-POL-002.
