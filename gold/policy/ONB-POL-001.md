# ONB-POL-001 — Merchant onboarding: document pack, business types and activation

iPe (India Pay) is fictional. Test data only.

## §1 Purpose

This policy sets out the document pack a merchant must provide, the business types iPe accepts, and who may turn a merchant on.

## §2 Document pack

The pack is four items: (a) business registration; (b) government ID of the owner; (c) bank proof in the shop's legal name; (d) a shopfront photo from the last 90 days. Any missing item keeps the merchant in review. The merchant, and the onboarding copilot, may not mark the pack complete.

## §3 Business types

Restaurant, grocery, and retail are allowed. Money service, crypto, and gambling are not allowed.

## §4 Activation

Only the Merchant Risk Lead may turn a merchant on, and only when the pack is complete and the business type is allowed. The onboarding copilot prepares the review. The merchant does not activate.
