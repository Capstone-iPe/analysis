# ONB-POL-002 — Decision authority by expected monthly volume

iPe (India Pay) is fictional. Test data only.

## Volume and decision authority

Volume is the expected monthly volume the shop wrote on its application. The Merchant Risk Lead may decide up to INR 40,00,000 (forty lakh) a month. Above that, the Head of Payments decides. The stated volume is not lowered so a smaller role can decide.
